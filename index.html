Hair care

Keep it glowing

A few general tips to help your color and extensions look their best between visits.

01Wait to wash

Give fresh color a couple of days before the first shampoo, and reach for sulfate-free, color-safe products.

02Tone & gloss

Blondes can turn brassy over time. A purple shampoo now and then, plus regular gloss appointments, keeps tone fresh.

03Turn down the heat

Always use a heat protectant and keep hot tools on a moderate setting to protect lightened hair.

04Extension love

Brush gently from ends to roots, sleep with hair in a loose braid, and keep up with move-up appointments.

These are general tips. You’ll get aftercare advice personalized to your hair at your appointment.
